## Our Project: *Side Streets*

Description: *Side Streets* is a narrative‑driven pixel exploration RPG where the player returns to their small hometown after years away. By exploring familiar locations, interacting with old friends, and uncovering forgotten memories, the player pieces together the life of a childhood friend who recently passed away. Daytime focuses on exploration and discovery, while nighttime features symbolic turn‑based dream battles that reflect the character’s emotional state and personal journey.

## My Focus: Level Design

For this project, I’m focusing on **level design**. My goal is to make the town feel natural to explore and emotionally connected to the story. I want players to understand where to go without needing big arrows or UI telling them what to do. Instead, the layout itself should guide them. What I want to make perfect is the **flow and feel** of each area like how players move through the town, how the environment supports the mood of the story, and how daytime areas contrast with the dream‑sequence levels at night. This is different from my teammates' focuses and goes beyond simply finishing Project I.

### What I want to make perfect
- Smooth, readable navigation through the town  
- Clear player guidance without relying on heavy UI  
- Emotional contrast between daytime exploration and night time dream levels   
- Environments that feel connected to the character’s past and the game’s themes
- The one most important thing I want is making this game so fun for players
